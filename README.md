**in full:**
Hi! You might think I’m a normal cat with a normal life…I’m not! 

My owner was a clockmaker and I’ve always loved sleeping in his antique grandfather clock. But one day, as I was taking my afternoon nap, the clock went haywire and exploded!

Now, I experience time differently and can manipulate relativity.

However, I miss being able to spend time with my owner..so I’m on a journey to find pieces of that grandfather clock and fix it. To do so, we need to collect up to 25 wooden chips to restore the clock. Please help me!

Be careful of the guardians. Use my ability of time dilation to avoid them! You will see two of me: the observed version of me and the real-time version of me. The real-time​ version of me is what I should be going at at non-relativistic speed. So, while moving super fast will get us past those guardians, we also need to be able to get our wooden chips quickly so that when you return to your real-time self, you dont get caught by them!!
## where to play
https://of-knee.itch.io/que-hora-es
- play with an x-box controller using the axis controls (plus sign), [X] to next text, [A] button to dilate time
- play with a computer using the WASD, [X] to next text, [SPACE BAR] button to dilate time

## The aim of the game
collect wood twin

## Controls
- [dilate time] - [space bar / A]
- [next text] - [x / X]
- [move] - [WASD / Axis controls (the plus button)]
